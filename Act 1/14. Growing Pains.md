### 14\. Growing Pains

*\[QUINN and DAVE at work at the RobotMe HQ\]*

**DAVE**: (breathless) Quinn\! Our metrics are going crazy right now\! The first batch sold out – three thousand robots in 15 minutes\! 

	**♪ GROWING PAINS ♪**

***QUINN:***  
*RobotMe, 3am*  
*We’re running out of space*  
*Pieces falling into place*

***DAVE:***  
*We need to up the pace*  
*Before we lose this race*  
*I know just what do*   
*I know who we can call*

*Dad has space that we can rent*  
*To scale at DuCharme Defense*  
*And once he sees what we release*  
*He’ll realize he was wrong about me*

**QUINN:**  
Dave, you want to give half of the money we raised to your dad to manufacture the robots? Imagine how that will look.

**DAVE:**  
Quinn, I’m just trying to save company money\!

**QUINN:**   
Since when did you start caring so much about saving us money? You’ve been the one spending money *carelessly* ever since we raised. The Tesla, the treadmill standing desk you never use, RobotMe branded pickleball rackets…

**DAVE:**  
pickleball is an investment in team bonding, Liz and I play daily. 

**QUINN:**  
Speaking of you and Liz – is there something going on there? 

**DAVE:**  
What, what do you mean?

**QUINN**  
You left your mic unmuted after our standup meeting yesterday – we heard everything.   
   
**DAVE:**  
Uh it was just our weekly 1:1…

**QUINN:**  
 Dave, she’s on our board\! It’s unprofessional.

**DAVE**:  
Can we focus? We need to manufacture 3,000 robots and fast. 

*\[QUINN shakes her head and leaves\]*

**DAVE**  
Hey Dad \-

*(listening, then defensive)*  
What? No, I’m not doing business school. 

*(trying to sound confident)*  
Actually it’s going great, we just raised $10 million.

*(cuts off, listening)*  
You don’t even know Quinn, she’s quite brilliant. 

*(pause)*  
Listen, I was calling to see if you could provide us with capacity at the DuCharme plant, things are scaling quickly.

*(pause)*  
What do you mean? Why does there always have to be something in it for you?

*(pause)*  
they're household robots. They're not built for... that.

(looks around for QUINN)  
I'll...think about it.

*\[Line goes dead. Dave stares at the phone and contemplates. Dave turns, seeing AVA in the doorway\]*

*(suddenly self-conscious)*  
Ava… What the hell are you looking at? 

*(pause, then more to himself than to Ava)*  
Someone has to make the hard decisions around here. That’s what it takes to be the best, to change the world. Right ?
