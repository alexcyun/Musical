### 16\. GreeneMachine

*\[GreeneMachine HQ. GORDON paces while BILL examines a RobotMe robot.\]*

**GORDON:**   
*(on phone)*   
Bill, do you know the key to greatness?

**BILL:**  
Uh…

**GORDON:**  
Struggle. For example, sometimes I fly economy instead of business. Once, I took the BART instead of Uber. Permission to give you some feedback?

***BILL:***  
Sure, I guess?

***GORDON:***   
*You* need to start putting in hundred hour workweeks. Stack that with cognitive enhancements like adderall, Zyn, L-theanine, and creatine, and then, you'll have a shot at greatness.

*\[Gordon takes a puff of vape smoke\]*

**BILL:**  
But you were out skiing all last week.

**GORDON:**  
That’s why I hired you\! Good leadership is all about delegating. Bill, stop thinking like an employee, where’s that ownership mindset?

**BILL:**   
*(examining a disassembled RobotMe unit on the desk)*  
Gordon, this RobotMe hardware is actually pretty impressive. We have 5,000 units shipping next quarter but no working prototype. We’re basically lying –

**GORDON:**   
*(waving hand dismissively)*   
Bill, it’s not a lie, it’s a pre-truth.

**BILL:**   
What are we going to do for the landing page? We need a picture, something.

*\[GORDON slaps a GreeneMachine sticker on a RobotMe unit\]*

**GORDON:**  
Here, good to go.

**BILL:**   
*(concerned)*   
Isn't this a bit... deceptive? 

**GORDON:**   
We gotta "fake it till you make it," Bill\! This is Silicon Valley\! 

*(checking phone)*   
TechCrunch just covered us: "GreeneMachine Raises $5B to Revolutionize Home Robotics"

**BILL:**   
The waitlist just hit 10,000\!

**GORDON:**   
See? Nobody cares if the product is trash. We're not just building robots, we're building HYPE\! By the way, Bill, I need your help drafting a post explaining our breakthrough AI technology.

**BILL:**   
What breakthrough AI technology?

**GORDON:**    
The one you're going to build in the next 30 days\! Think big, Bill\! 

**BILL**:   
*(working on a robot when suddenly it starts smoking)*  
Gordon, we’re on fire\!

**GORDON**:   
You bet we are\! 10,000 pre-orders already, RobotMe is smoked\!

**BILL:**  
No, I mean our prototype is literally on fire\! That’s the 3rd burnt circuit board this week. Maybe we should delay the launch –

**GORDON:**   
And let RobotMe get even further ahead? 

**BILL**:  
But our robot can barely move without catching fire...

**GORDON**:   
*(shouting)*   
Then MAKE IT NOT CATCH ON FIRE\!

**BILL:**  
*(sighs in deep exasperation)*  
I’ll add it to the roadmap, I think we can get to it in Q4…of 2032\.

*\[Prototype robot starts spinning wildly\]*

Get down\!

*\[Robot explodes in a shower of sparks. As smoke clears, GORDON stares at wreckage and holds his head in his hands\]*
