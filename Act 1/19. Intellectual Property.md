So stupid. God – Dave and Liz, I can’t believe…

Quinn,  there’s one more thing.

What?

**LIZ:**   
You still have some company property.

**QUINN:**  
Here’s my keycard. Now go.

*\[QUINN hands over a keycard\]*

**LIZ:**  
There’s something *else*.  
*(gesturing to Ava)*

Ava, no\!

Quinn, help me\!  Liz, what are you doing? Where am I going?
