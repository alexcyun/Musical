### 8\. Pitch Practice

*\[DAVE is passed out in a ball pit with a whippet canister\]*

**QUINN:**  
Dave\! DAVE\! Wake up\! Startup Spotlight is in THREE HOURS and we haven't practiced our pitch once\!

**DAVE:**  
*(groaning, barely opening his eyes)*  
Quinn... we're gonna be fine. Relax.

**QUINN:**  
Relax? RELAX? Our entire future depends on this pitch\! Building robots is expensive, if we don’t raise money we're DEAD in the water\! Put this on.

*\[QUINN hands DAVE a suit jacket\]*

**DAVE:**  
I can’t wear that. Listen, Quinn, we're in the Valley now.  
*(stands unsteadily)*   
If you dress too nicely, people get... suspicious. You can’t try too hard.

**QUINN:**  
So what, I should roll out of bed like you did?

**DAVE**:  
*(putting his hands on her shoulders, patronizing)*  
No, see, that's the thing. You still have to try, but you have to make it look like you aren't trying. I don’t make the rules.

**QUINN:**  
*(pushing his hands away)*  
That's the most ridiculous thing I've ever heard.

**DAVE:**  
*(cutting her off)*  
Welcome to the Valley\! *(checks phone)* It’s time to go\!
