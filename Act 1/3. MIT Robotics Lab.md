### 3\. MIT Robotics Lab

*\[Robotics equipment and whiteboards filled with equations. PROFESSOR ROSS stands at a whiteboard, mid-lecture, surrounded by STUDENTS including QUINN and DAVE\]*

**PROFESSOR ROSS:**   
...and with the latest advances in attention mechanisms, we're seeing robots that can learn from limited training data, outperforming humans on most benchmarks. Soon, they’ll be teaching us. Who knows, maybe I’ll be out of a job. Don’t get your hopes up, I have tenure. 

Anyway, we have some new students joining us…

*\[QUINN rushes in, breathless, clutching AVA to her chest.\]*  
   
**QUINN:**   
Hi, Professor Ross. Sorry I'm late\! My alarm didn't go off, and then I went to the wrong building—

**PROFESSOR ROSS:**  
*(cutting her off)*   
Well, you're just in time to introduce yourself.

**QUINN:**  
I’m a first year student interested in building household companion robots. For the last couple years, I've been building Ava.  
*(holds up AVA)*   
I taught her to help my mom clean the house.

**PROFESSOR ROSS:**   
*(raising an eyebrow)*.  
That sounds like a cute…toy. But in my lab we keep it technical \-  computer vision, planning, simulation… 

**QUINN**:  
*(defensive)*  
Well, I *am* using a lot of those techniques and –

**PROFESSOR ROSS:**   
What about you, Dave? Wait. Dave DuCharme? Isn’t your dad –

**DAVE**:   
CEO of DuCharme Defense, yes.

Wow, well it’s great to have you.

I heard MIT has the best robotics program.

**QUINN:**   
*(Snaps back)*  
Oh, and I’m sure Daddy’s donations had nothing to do with your acceptance.

Enough bickering\! Dave, tell us about yourself.
