### 10\. Rejection

*\[QUINN, DAVE, and LIZ at the group house.\]*

**QUINN**  
What are we going to do without any funding? Maybe we should just give up and go back to MIT. 

**DAVE**  
Quinn, we’re not giving up that easily.

**LIZ:**   
*(brewing coffee, filling up a mug)*   
So, how did Startup Spotlight go? 

**QUINN:**   
Not good. Gordon called Ava a toaster on wheels. (sighs)

**DAVE:**   
Investors just don’t get our vision. They want us to build another enterprise AI data cloud whatever.

**LIZ:**   
*(taking a thoughtful sip)*   
You know what you two need? A night off. Rachel's throwing a party over on Fulton.

**QUINN**:   
*(frowning)*  
I should work on the cognitive algorithms tonight.

**LIZ:**   
Come on Quinn. You never know where you might meet. 

**DAVE**:   
(hand landing lightly on QUINN's shoulder)   
I heard Sergey Brin was at the last one.

**QUINN**:   
Fine. But I'm not staying late.

**LIZ**:  
*(raising her glass)*   
Rejection is a rite of passage in the valley.

**AVA**:   
Statistically, successful startups are rejected an average of 7.3 times before securing funding.

*\[All three smile and turn to look at AVA in surprise.\]*

**DAVE**:   
*(laughing)*   
See? Even Ava gets it. 

*(already heading for the door)*   
Come on guys, the Waymo’s tired of waiting.
