###  11\. FOMO

*\[LIZ, DAVE, and QUINN arrive at the party, music pulsates and lights flash, but a sign indicates “ALCOHOL-FREE NETWORKING MIXER SPONSORED BY SALESFORCE”. People are standing around talking awkwardly and not dancing.\]*

**QUINN:**   
Are all parties in San Francisco like this? 

**DAVE**:   
Yeah, how am I supposed to take my mind off things without alcohol?

**LIZ:**   
Two words. Meditation…and mushrooms. You know, I’ve been in your shoes, I *get* that being a founder isn’t easy. I was fortunate to sell my company and get out of that rat race. Now I just run the Group House and my Burning Man camp. 

**QUINN:**  
Is that what you do all day then?

**LIZ:**  
Preparing for Burning Man is basically a full time job\! You have to prepare your camp, build the art, arrange transportation for the art, transport the art, tear down the art, go to several decompression parties, and by the time you are done with that, it's time to start preparing for next year\!

*\[QUINN walks to the corner of the stage, observing the party\]*

**DAVE:**  
Wow, you sound really busy. Liz, investors just don’t get our business, maybe we should…give up.

**♪ FOMO ♪**  
*\[LIZ interacts flirtatiously with DAVE, holding his hand, guiding him through the party\]*

***LIZ:***   
*You know Dave, I raised $10m for my company. Let me teach you a thing or two about fundraising.*

*You seem like a really nice guy, but that’s not gonna fly with VCs*  
*When it comes to raising money, I’ll give you my tips and you’ll see*  
*One: The more you push them away the more they’re willing to pay, it’s true*  
*Two: Don’t raise in August or July, all the VCs are in Europe that time*  
*Three: They don’t care if your product is trash, if you’re not making any cash, that’s fine*  
*But this is key \- you need to build some hype*  
*Post online and get some likes*  
*Do some promo, create FOMO\!*

*\[DAVE with a look of understanding…\]*

***LIZ and DAVE:***  
*Four \- You gotta shop it around, if they miss out on the round, they’ll get laughed out of town*

***LIZ**:*   
*Are you getting it, Dave?*  
*If you want to raise, it’s FOMO you need to create*

*(turning to Quinn)*   
*Quinn \- I think I know why our pitch isn’t working\!*

***QUINN**:*   
*Oh yeah, why?*

***DAVE***  
*Let me explain.*   
*Quinn \- We need a brand new approach, but lucky for you I can be your coach.*  
*Quinn you’re just too nice and polite, I get you want to be liked, but not this time*  
*When it comes to raising money, this might sound kinda funny, but you gotta make a VC cry*

***LIZ**:*   
*That’s not what I said\!*

***DAVE:***   
*You gotta act like you’re too busy, I learned this all from Lizzy. So let’s try it out\!*
