###  18\. Board Meeting Coup  

*\[QUINN, DAVE, and LIZ, sit around the table\]*

	**♪ BOARD MEETING RAP BATTLE ♪**

**DAVE**:  
Hey everyone, we’ll go ahead and start our Q2 board meeting. 

**QUINN:**  
First on the agenda \- we made a breakthrough in emotional intelligence that we’ll be rolling out over the next few months.  
   
***LIZ:***  
*That’s neat but I’m afraid to admit*  
*It looks like growth has slowed to less than 2%*  
*Competition from Gordon is really taking a hit*  
*Tell us what are you planning to do about all this?*

***QUINN:***  
*Well, the main reason, the rollout’s delayed*  
*Is the current technology is not yet safe*  
*Gordon’s robots are out there literally catching fire\!*

***LIZ:***  
*But did you see the NASA engineer he just hired?*

***DAVE**:*  
*The real reason that we’ve gone off track*  
*Is Quinn turning down multi-million dollar contracts*

***LIZ:***  
*Quinn is that true, how do you justify that fact*  
*We need someone to lead whose not afraid to act*

***QUINN:***  
*Dave, I thought you got the mission, the vision*  
*Traded out your values for greed and ambition. A military transition?*   
*We started this company to help people who need assistance.*  
*Not to sell out for more business*

***LIZ:***  
*Quinn, I know you want to do what’s right*  
*But sometimes doing what’s right means stepping aside*  
*The numbers tell a story that we just can’t deny*  
*For RobotMe to thrive*  
*We need you  to step aside*

***QUINN:***  
*(in disbelief)*  
*Are you kidding me?*  
*You must be out of your mind*  
*I started this company*  
*The idea was mine*

***LIZ**:*  
*All in favor? Say aye*

*\[LIZ raises her hand, glancing at Dave, who raises his hand\]*

**QUINN:**  
Dave? You too?

*(Dave looks at her sadly and raises his hand.)*

***LIZ:***   
Motion passed.   
Quinn today will be your last  
As CTO

as research engineer

(smiling) Respectfully, fuck you, Liz. Though I guess Dave’s already doing that.

***LIZ:***
