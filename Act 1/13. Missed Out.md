### 13\. Missed Out

*\[Coffee shop. QUINN is sitting with her laptop open, GORDON enters.\]*

**GORDON**:   
(loudly)   
Quinn. We need to talk.

*\[QUINN looks up\]*

**QUINN**:   
Gordon. I don't recall scheduling a meeting.

**GORDON**:   
(diving straight in)  
I was wrong about RobotMe. You’re gonna change the world, I know it.

**QUINN**:   
And coincidentally, you realized this one day after our oversubscribed seed round? 

**GORDON**:   
I…look – I promise I can be a value-add investor. 

**QUINN**:  
Didn’t you say that Ava belongs in the trash?

**AVA:**  
I can confirm, Gordon did place me into a waste receptacle.

**GORDON**:   
*(wincing)*  
Quinn—I can't miss another unicorn or I’ll never raise another fund and my career will be over. Name your terms.

**QUINN**:  
*(closes laptop and stands up)*  
The round is closed, Gordon.

*(GORDON sighs and tears up)*

*\[GORDON center stage, QUINN exits\]*

**BILL:**  
Gordon, it was really brave of you to share your struggles so openly. You know, as part of the 1%, you’re part of an underrepresented community, since there’s just so few of you.

**GORDON:**  
Bill, this is exactly why we’re friends after all these years. We’ve had so many good times together. Like the time I paid you to do my math homework in high school. Anyway, how’s academia?

**BILL:**  
I’ve spent the last 15 years researching robotics and can barely afford rent in the Bay Area. Meanwhile, my students’ signing bonuses are more than I’ll make in a lifetime.

**GORDON:**  
Bill, we can either ride the wave, or get crushed by it. With your technical skills and my business genius… 

**BILL:**  
Are you suggesting we get into the robotics business? 

**GORDON:**  
Yes\! I would like that but, I don’t know…allocating capital is humanity’s highest calling. Plus if you’re not very good at it, no one finds out for a decade.

*\[Blackout\]*
