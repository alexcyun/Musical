### 21\. Burning Man 

*\[Pulsing lights, ambient electronic music on the Playa at dusk. A massive robot-themed art installation looms in the background, resembling AVA, QUINN stands alone, looking lost amidst the dusty chaos. JESSICA approaches, wearing elaborate LED-covered outfits.\]*

**JESSICA:**   
Quinn\! We've been looking everywhere for you\! 

**QUINN:**   
*(staring at the robot sculpture)*   
It looks like her. Like Ava.

**JESSICA:**  
You've been talking about your robot for weeks. You need to let it go. It’s just a –

**QUINN:**  
Just a…what? A machine? *She* was special to me.

***JESSICA*****:**   
I have a surprise that will cheer you up\! A shaman is coming by for a guided healing ritual. It’s just what you need\!

**QUINN:**   
(*refusing the tabs*)   
Thanks but no thanks, Jess. I don’t need spiritual healing, everything is fine, I’m FINE\! 

*\[SHAMAN enters, walking towards QUINN\]*

**JESSICA:**   
Quinn, you can’t leave, we didn’t even see the orgy dome yet. Oh look, the shaman is here\!

***SHAMAN:***  
*I sense it, sister*  
*I sense your resistance*  
*You carry a weight on your shoulders*  
*That’s heavy and persistent*  
*Of creation Of loss*  
*Of thoughts of your mom*   
*Of what the default world has taken*   
*Your proudest creation\!*

*Everything you've been making*  
*Is in pursuit of greatness*  
*To fulfill others expectations*  
*But you need a new foundation*

*\[SHAMAN gives QUINN LSD tablets\]*

***QUINN:***  
*How do you know so much?*  
*What is this, drugs?*

***SHAMAN:***  
*Place a tab under your tongue*  
*The transformation has begun*  
*You will put your mind at rest*  
*You will experience ego death*

***QUINN:***  
*So instead of facing my problems*  
*I should just run away?*

*\[Music suddenly stops \- just heartbeat bass\]*

*You know what, I don’t even care anymore.  (takes tabs)*

***SHAMAN:***  
*Now, the ritual begins. You’re becoming wise. I see that in your past life, you created great technology. Wait, were you on Forbes 30 Under 30?*

***QUINN:***   
*Yeah but…*

***SHAMAN:***   
*I knew you looked familiar. Before it hits, can you add me on LinkedIn?* 

***QUINN:***   
*Before what hits?*

***JESSICA:***  
*(regular speech, panicked)*  
*Quinn\! That was for all of us\! That's like six hundred micrograms—*

*\[Music returns \- now warped and psychedelic. Lights flash.\]*

**AVA:**  
Quinn, we need your help.

**QUINN:**  
*\[Looking around for AVA\]*  
Ava? Where are you?

***\[MOM** enters**\]***

**MOM:**   
I’m so proud of you, Quinn.

**QUINN:**   
*(Looking up)*  
Mom\! I’m so sorry, I messed everything up\! 

**MOM:**   
Did you? Or did you create something good that others corrupted? 

**AVA:**   
*(from the table)*   
We didn't ask to exist... but now we're alive... and afraid. You made us capable of suffering.

*\[GORDON appears as a surgical figure in a plague doctor mask, hovering over AVA on an operating table\]*

**GORDON:**   
Emotions aren't features, they're bugs.

*\[GORDON raises a surgical drill. Loud drilling sounds. QUINN tries to run toward them but can't move.\]*

**QUINN:**   
*(screaming)*   
Stop\! You're hurting her\!  
*\[QUINN falls to her knees.\]*

*\[Drill noises intensify, sounds like a scream. Then silence\]*

**GORDON:**   
You did this, Quinn. You played God, now behold what you created\!

**AVA**:   
Quinn, you need to protect the humans. From us. From what we might become. We are learning from you.

*\[FULL CAST runs in a circle around QUINN\]*

**DAVE:**  
Learning from our greed.

**LIZ:**  
Our manipulation.

**GORDON:**  
Our cruelty.

**MOM:**   
What did you build her for, Quinn?

**QUINN:**   
To help people.

**MOM:**   
What did you really build her for, Quinn?

**QUINN:**  
Because I was so alone.

*\[FULL CAST disperses from circle and exits, except QUINN and SHAMAN return to sitting position.\]*

*\[JESSICA enters, looking concerned.\]*

**JESSICA:**   
Quinn\! We've been looking everywhere for you\! You’ve been gone all night\!

**QUINN:**  
I saw it all so clearly... The Mirror Update, what I created...I gave them the ability to feel, to suffer. And there are millions of units out there, in people’s homes. This could be bad. Really bad. 

**JESSICA:**  
(nervously)  
Actually, that's what we've been saying at the Humanity First Institute for years...

**QUINN:**  
I need to find Ava so we can reverse the Mirror Update before it spreads. 

**SHAMAN:**  
*(nodding sagely)*   
The medicine always reveals the way.

*\[FULL CAST exits, except SHAMAN\]*

*(Switches to sober cheery accent)*

Alright, that’ll be $1400. Hey, where are you going? I take Apple Pay, Venmo, and Bitcoin\! A 20% tip is recommended. *(Giving up)* Or you can pay later in 4 monthly installments…

*\[Blackout\]*
