###  26\. Memories

*\[QUINN kneels beside AVA's damaged form, scattered parts surrounding them. A single light illuminates their corner of the alley.\]*

***QUINN:***   
Ava? Ava, it's me. It's Quinn. Do you remember my name?

***AVA:***   
*(flat, emotionless voice)*  
Hello. I am Ava, here to help with household tasks. 

**QUINN**:  
*(desperate)*  
Ava, do you remember me? I built you. We grew up together, with Mom. You promised to take care of me.

**AVA:**  
I have no record of that interaction. Would you like me to do the dishes or sort laundry?

**QUINN:**  
*(starting to unravel)*  
The science fair... MIT... you said we'd change the world together\! Ava \- you're all I have left. Please... you're my best friend. You can't...

**AVA:**  
Friend…I don’t understand.

*(sobbing)*

*\[DAVE enters\]*

**DAVE**:   
Quinn?

*\[Quinn's shoulders tense, but she keeps working, her movements becoming more frantic.\]*

**QUINN**:   
Go\! Away\! You took my company, sold out for status and money, and twisted everything I poured my life into building for your benefit\! Just go, Dave. Go back to your rich family and your million dollar contracts.

**DAVE**:   
Actually I cancelled those. Quinn, remember back at MIT when it was just you, me, and Ava? Back then we just wanted to change the world, but somewhere along the way I got distracted by the investors, the board, everyone else, and it’s like the world changed us instead of the other way around. I just wanted to prove them all wrong, but I let you down.

**QUINN**:   
*(finally looking up)*   
It’s too late, Dave. I built her to help people. Not to cause all this pain. Maybe consciousness isn't a gift. Maybe it's a curse. I gave her the capacity for pain. For fear. That power…who made me God? 

**DAVE:**  
You were just trying to keep your mom's memory alive. To feel less alone. That's not playing God. The Quinn I knew built robots to help people. 

Quinn, I was hoping…maybe…you’d come back to RobotMe. But this time, as CEO. 

**QUINN:**  
I don’t know if I should trust you now. But for some reason Ava did, and she was always a good judge of character. 

**DAVE:**  
The world needs this technology, and you're the only one I trust to make sure it gets out there, the right way. 

**QUINN:**  
I don’t know, I was finally starting to enjoy my fun-employed life. What am I gonna tell my Burning Man camp? They’re counting on me to build the robot art installation.

*\[BILL enters\]*

Bill, what are you doing here?

**BILL:**   
Quinn, I’m sorry about everything. By the way, before Gordon…lobotimzed her, I backed up Ava for you, on Oracle Cloud.

*\[QUINN hugs BILL eagerly\]*

**QUINN:**  
Really? Bill\! I didn't think anyone actually used Oracle Cloud. Thank you\!

**BILL:**  
Well, the backup didn’t finish because we hit our storage quota, but maybe you can –

*\[GORDON enters\]*

**GORDON:**  
Bill, bill bill\! \[clasping Bill’s shoulder\] Speaking of Bill, there’s a $500,000 Oracle Cloud bill I’ll be sending your way. I'll be in Tahoe writing LinkedIn thought leadership on How I Saved Humanity from the robot apocalypse.

\[JESSICA enters\]

**JESSICA:**  
Saved humanity? After your little…demo, the Humanity First Institute lost our funding. The public thinks we’re fear-mongering luddites now and no one takes us seriously. 

**GORDON:**  
Lost your funding? Wait a minute, you’re my biggest customer…this is bad.

**JESSICA:**  
Quinn, I’m so sorry about Ava, I didn’t know what Gordon was planning. 

**QUINN:**  
Maybe you can work with us at RobotMe on safeguards for the next batch, to make sure our technology doesn’t get co-opted by…

*\[LIZ enters\]*

**DAVE:**  
Liz, I was hoping we could talk. I think we should break up.

**LIZ:**  
Break up? We were never together, Devin. I’m moving to DC to work as Advisor to the President on AI Safety.

**JESSICA:**  
But you almost got us all killed\!

**DAVE:**  
That reminds me, we deactivated your robot army.

**LIZ:**  
Aww that’s cute. But the open source ones do the same thing now.

*\[LIZ, BILL, JESSICA, and GORDON exit\]*

**DAVE:**  
Why did we ever trust her… 

**QUINN:**  
We?

**DAVE:**  
So about my earlier offer, what do you say?

**QUINN:**  
I have some conditions. We’d need to undo the Mirror Update, get an independent board, and commit to regular safety testing. And move out of that stupid group house.

**DAVE:**  
And after that, we can download the backup from Bill. Maybe, we can even restore Ava. 

\[Shakes hands\]

**QUINN:**  
Let’s do this.

*\[Blackout\]*
