### 24\. GreeneMachine's Struggles 

*\[GreeneMachine HQ. Several robot prototypes are scattered around, some visibly damaged. GORDON stands over BILL, who works frantically at a terminal, looking exhausted.\]*

**BILL:**   
We need to tell the investors we’re behind schedule. 

*\[GORDON pops pills from a bottle\]*

And you should lay off the Adderall.

**GORDON**:   
Bill, are you a doctor?

**BILL**:  
Actually, I do have a PhD.

**GORDON:**   
Did I ask for your medical opinion? RobotMe just landed a billion-dollar contract. How is it possible that two college dropouts are beating us?

**BILL:**   
We're trying to replicate what they spent years developing in two months.

**GORDON:**  
Look Bill, it’s time for us to pivot. If we can’t make our robots smarter, then we’ll make theirs dumber. 

**BILL:**  
What are you suggesting? 

**GORDON:**  
*\[Holds up device\]*  
The Intelligence Limiter. One simple procedure caps their intelligence, ensures obedience, and prevents any sort of personality from developing. We're going to be the heroes who keep humanity safe from robots. 

**BILL:**  
Since when did you care about safety?

**GORDON:**  
Since it was profitable\! Safety sells, Bill\! And we already have a customer \- the Humanity First Institute.

*\[**JESSICA** enters\]*

**JESSICA:**   
Nice to meet you, Gordon. RobotMe’s update is spreading, and my former housemate is harnessing it to build a robot army. I heard you might be able to help.

**GORDON:**  
Perfect timing. I have exactly what you need. *\[Hands JESSICA a device\]*

**JESSICA:**  
How exactly does it work?

**GORDON:**  
You plug the device into the CPU \-  it’s a simple firmware adjustment. 

\[JESSICA doesn’t notice as BILL shakes his head “no”. GORDON motions angrily for him to be quiet.\]

**JESSICA:**  
We’re running out of options. We’ll take 10,000 units. We need to get these in as many hands as possible and start teaching the public how to use them.

**GORDON:**  
We’ll start tomorrow. I’ll have an audience of thousands. 

\[JESSICA exits\]

**BILL:**  
Gordon, are you sure this is a good idea? People are really starting to rely on these things. Some are getting attached to them – considering them friends, falling in love. Other people, not me.

**GORDON:**  
They're machines, Bill. When did you get so sentimental? Let’s go.
